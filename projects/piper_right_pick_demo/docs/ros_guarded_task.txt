Each JointState has exactly six positions in radians, velocity [0,0,0,0,0,0,1],
and empty effort. All targets are manufacturer-nominal, using exact millidegree
encoding. The initial stage is commissioning: one admitted common-ratio segment

100ms complete raw feedback, healthy enabled state, and unchanged jaw. These
limits also apply throughout task stage. They are not a collision certificate.

Normal action sends one four-frame J transaction, then observes actual arrival
and three seconds of new stable feedback. Ordinary completed actions may be
followed by the next explicitly selected action in task stage.

~hold_status returns sequence, adoption_token, generation, stage, active, phase,
hold_service, result, result_sha256, resume_service and failure. Send at most one
Trigger to the exact current hold_service (.../hold_current/seq_N_TOKEN). Accepted

Only the existing worker sends the hold, after finishing the initial transaction.
Ownership checks and durable intent precede the final sample. All six hold targets
come from one newest complete snapshot; another immediate witness must still
meet the unchanged 0.003rad/0.5mm/0.003rad slip checks. No blocking logging or graph
query intervenes. Both original motion box and local hold box remain enforced.
The worker sends at most four replacement frames and observes three seconds of
new stable arrival. Recorded maxima are discrete samples, not continuous braking
distance guarantees. A crash between durable reservation and exact receipt is
unresolved, never evidence of zero transmission or permission to retry.

Commissioning is promoted only after at least two axes show actual recent motion,
>=0.2 degree progress and remaining distance at the hold sample, and >=0.2 degree
final separation from the original goal. A natural arrival, early cancellation,
or single moving axis does not promote. The service never delays stopping to
achieve this experiment window. A suitable external request must be chosen by
the supervisor (for the current proportional J2/J3 segment, J2 about0.35degree
progress gives more margin than0.25degree for J3's progress criterion).

After a successful hold, adoption is revoked in software and subsequent goals
are blocked. The supervisor reads action_N_result.json and verifies the result
before calling the unique resume_service. Its path binds sequence, generation,
adoption token, and full result SHA. Resume acquires the action lock, verifies
unchanged result and complete initial/hold receipts, rechecks ownership and fresh
three-second stationary state, then archives the old stopped generation and
creates a new task generation. It sends ZERO actuator frames. Old resume and
hold endpoints cannot control the new generation. Failures, partial transmissions,
pending outcomes and unqualified commissioning cannot resume through this route.

Client timeout/exit itself does not stop the robot. A client may request the bound
hold while fresh healthy feedback is available and wait for its confirmed result.
The driver's own 120-second arrival timeout latches failure; it does not blindly
send a stop. Hold observation has its own20-second deadline. Fault/stale/partial
conditions lock further commands without quickstop/reset/disable/retry.
